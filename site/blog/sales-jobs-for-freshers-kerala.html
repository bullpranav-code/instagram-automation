Getting your first sales job in Kerala

Accent · 4 November 2026 · 4 min read

Sales is one of the most open doors for freshers: many companies hire for attitude and communication, then train on the product. Here is how to walk in prepared.

Common entry-level roles

  • Sales Executive — meets customers in a showroom, store or field.
  • Business Development Executive (BDE) — finds and follows up leads, often by phone.
  • Inside Sales / Tele-sales — sells over calls and WhatsApp.
  • Sales Coordinator — supports the sales team with orders and records.

What interviewers check

  • Can you speak clearly and confidently for two minutes?
  • Do you listen before you pitch?
  • Can you handle a "No" calmly?
  • Will you follow up without being pushy?

A one-week plan

  1. Day 1–2: learn features vs benefits. Pick any product at home and write five benefits.
  2. Day 3: write your "Tell me about yourself" answer.
  3. Day 4: practise "Sell me this pen": ask a question first, then pitch one benefit.
  4. Day 5: prepare answers to "Too costly" and "I'll think about it".
  5. Day 6: record a mock call and review it.
  6. Day 7: apply to 5 roles and follow up after two days.
