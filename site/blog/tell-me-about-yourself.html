How to answer "Tell me about yourself" in an interview

Accent · 4 November 2026 · 4 min read

Almost every interview starts with this question, and the first minute sets the mood for the rest. The good news: you can prepare it once and use it everywhere.

The 4-step formula

  1. Present — who you are now.
  2. Past — your education or experience that matters for this job.
  3. Strengths — one or two strengths, with a small proof.
  4. Future — what you want next, and why this role fits.

A sample answer

Good morning. My name is Anjali, and I am from Kozhikode. I recently completed my B.Com from Calicut University. In my final year I did a three-month internship at a trading company, where I handled billing and customer records. My strengths are that I learn quickly and I communicate well with customers. I am now looking for an opportunity to grow in accounts and customer service, and I believe this role is a great fit for me.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Saying "Myself Anjali". Say "My name is Anjali."
  • Reading your whole CV. Keep it under 60 seconds.
  • Talking about family and hobbies that have nothing to do with the job.

Practise it today

Write your own answer with the four steps, say it in front of a mirror three times, then record it. Slow down, smile, and pause between the steps.
