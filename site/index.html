How the Green Task works

  1. Join any Accent course
  2. Plant a seed or sapling at home, school or college
  3. Post a photo on Instagram and tag Accent
  4. Upload the photo in the Accent app and earn Leaf Points
  5. Keep caring for it and share a photo every month

Questions

Good to know

How much do the courses cost?

Each short course is ₹149. The first lessons of every course are free to watch in the Accent app.

Which language are the courses in?

Sales Basics, AI Tools and Interview English are explained in Malayalam with English terms. Hindi Express teaches spoken Hindi with English captions.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Pass the final quiz with 7 out of 10 and download your certificate in the app.

How do I pay and join?

Open a course in the app, tap "Pay & join" and message us on WhatsApp for the payment details. The Accent team approves your access, usually the same day.

Does Accent help with jobs?

Yes. Build a profile on the careers portal and apply to partner companies. The 30-day Sales Program includes a 14-day internship and placement support.
